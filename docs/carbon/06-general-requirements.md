# :material-format-list-checks: General Requirements

Field Maps and Survey123 are built to maintain compliance and data quality, but there
are a handful of general rules that sit outside the app steps.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    Minimum three samples per strata. Fill in every hole before moving on. Field codes
    follow Project ID, Sample Round, Sequence, for example `OCT001`.

## Sync as soon as you can

Once sampling for a project is complete, sync both Field Maps and Survey123 over a
stable data connection.

!!! warning "WARNING"
    Sync as soon as practical after finishing (as soon as a secure connection is
    available), so AgriProve's GIS administrator can confirm all data has uploaded
    before you demobilise. Any data issues are far easier to fix on-site than after you
    have left.

## Minimum sampling

1. Collect a minimum of three samples per strata. Cross-check this before
   demobilising.
2. Where a reserve point is used, it must be taken in the same strata as the unused
   primary sample point.

## Offsetting

3. If an obstruction (a tree, a boulder, and so on) prevents sampling at the target
   location, use the offsetting procedure:

    - Move north until the obstacle is cleared, to a maximum of 4 metres.
    - If that doesn't clear it, change direction in 15 degree increments until it does.
    - Never cross a strata boundary while offsetting.
    - See Section 1.2.b of the Supplement for the full basis of this procedure.

## Closing each hole

4. Fill in and close each hole before moving on to the next core. Collapse or fill the
   top of the hole with a shovel.

## Labelling and field codes

5. Label every sample bag with the field code, at minimum. A barcode firmly attached
   to the bag, and protected from being obscured in transit, is preferable. The field
   code format is:

    - Project ID, Sample Round, Sequence, for example `OCT001`.

## Measurement accuracy

6. Take every soil core measurement as accurately as possible, and make sure all
   recovered soil for each layer goes into the corresponding sample bag.

!!! danger "SWMS"
    Small errors in sample length and weight can cause large errors in downstream
    calculations, particularly bulk density.
