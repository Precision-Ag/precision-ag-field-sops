# :material-clipboard-text: Completing the Survey123 Form

The Survey123 form is specifically designed to capture everything required for a
compliant sampling round. Fields marked with a red asterisk are required.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    L1 must be exactly 30cm, never less. A valid sample is more than 60cm of total core
    length. Never adjust a sample length to account for core compression.

## Reserve points and project details

1. Answer the opening questions. If the sample you are collecting is **not** a reserve
   point, select **Yes**.
2. Enter the project name, the field code populates automatically.

## Location safety and accessibility

3. If your location is exactly where the sample must be extracted, and it is safely
   accessible, select **Yes** under **Is this sample point location safe and
   accessible?**

    a. If **No**, photographic evidence showing why the sample could not be extracted
       is required under the **Location of Record** section.
    b. If the sample can be extracted with an offset applied, follow the offsetting
       instructions in the form (see [General Requirements](06-general-requirements.md)),
       and photograph both the obstruction and the actual sampled location.

## Cutting shoe and push depth

4. Check and confirm the diameter of the cutting shoe for every sample. It can change
   as the shoe wears.
5. If there is no change, select **No** under **Check cutting shoe and equipment**. If
   any adjustment was needed, select **Yes** and note what was adjusted.
6. Enter the internal cutting shoe diameter, in centimetres, under **Extraction core
   diameter**. This must not be less than 3.8cm.
7. Enter the **Target soil push depth** in centimetres. This should always be 100cm
   unless AgriProve Operations has specified otherwise in writing.
8. Extract the soil core. Do not use lubricants, only water may be used to wash
   equipment between samples.
9. Enter the **Actual push depth** in centimetres. This is how far the probe was pushed
   into the ground, not the length of the recovered sample.

## Core layers

10. Extract the core from the tube and separate L1 (0 to 30cm) from L2 (30cm and
    beyond). Record the length of each layer accurately in Survey123.

!!! warning "WARNING"
    Small measurement errors here can have a significant impact on total carbon stock
    calculations. Take care with every measurement.

11. L1 must be exactly 30cm, and must not be less than 30cm. Do not adjust sample
    lengths to account for core compression.
12. Calculate the total length (L1 + L2) and enter it under **Total sample depth
    extracted**, in centimetres.

## Valid sample rules

13. If the total core length is greater than 60cm, continue completing the form and
    collect the samples.

    a. If between 30cm and 60cm total sample length was obtained, consider a second
       attempt at extracting the core from the same location (for example, move
       forward 20cm).
    b. If less than 30cm was extracted, consider a second attempt from the same
       location. If more than 30cm of total core length is still not obtained,
       consider the offsetting procedure to try for a compliant sample within the 4m
       buffer. If that still fails, use a reserve point.

| Total core length | Result |
| --- | --- |
| > 60cm | Valid sample, more than 60cm extracted |
| 30 to 60cm | Possibly valid, more than 30cm but less than 60cm extracted |
| < 30cm | Not valid, less than 30cm or other issue |

14. Capture the location of the soil core, with GNSS accuracy less than 1m. If
    capturing a non-sampled location, record where the observation was made from
    instead.

Once the core measurements are recorded, move on to
[Photos and Sign-off](04-photos-and-signoff.md) to complete the rest of the form.
