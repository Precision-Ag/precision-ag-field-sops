# :material-book-open-page-variant: Introduction

Physical soil sampling is a core component of AgriProve's soil carbon projects. It forms
the basis of estimating Soil Organic Carbon stocks, and this protocol must be followed
exactly to keep the project compliant. This manual is for Precision Ag field techs
sampling on AgriProve carbon projects.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    Sample using ArcGIS Field Maps, Survey123 and QuickCapture. Stay inside the 4m
    buffer zone, measure the core layers accurately, take every required photo, and get
    a valid sample of at least 30cm before moving on. When in doubt, stop and check with
    AgriProve rather than guessing.

## Scope and regulatory basis

This procedure has been developed to be compliant with:

- The Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Act 2011 (the CFI Act)
- The Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative) Regulations 2011
- The Carbon Credits (Carbon Farming Initiative, Estimation of Soil Organic Carbon
  Sequestration using Measurement and Models) Methodology Determination 2021 (the
  Method)
- The Explanatory Statement, authorised version registered 02/12/2021 to F2021L01696
- The Supplement to the Carbon Credits Carbon Farming Initiative, Estimation of Soil
  Organic Carbon Sequestration Using Measurement and Models Methodology Determination
  2021 (the Supplement)

This manual operationalises the sampling requirements set out in the Supplement. Every
tech following this manual should also have read and understood the Supplement itself
and how it interacts with these steps.

## Relationships

This protocol is implemented under AgriProve's Quality Management System. AgriProve's
Quality Policy is provided during contractor onboarding, and every sampling contractor
must agree to operate under it.

## Requirements

Access to the following AgriProve systems is required before mobilising. AgriProve's GIS
administrator issues login credentials:

- ArcGIS Field Maps
- ArcGIS Survey123
- ArcGIS QuickCapture

## Safety and biosecurity

Safety comes first. It is each tech's responsibility to work safely, in line with both
Precision Ag and AgriProve policies and the relevant state-based safe work legislation.

!!! danger "SWMS"
    Sample collection must never be attempted if it is unsafe to do so, no matter how
    close the site is to being finished.

Before commencing work on a project, the following must be complete. AgriProve's
Contractor Onboarding Checklist should be completed in full first.

1. Hold a current Work Health and Safety Policy, available to AgriProve on request.
2. Hold current workers compensation, professional indemnity, and public liability
   insurance, with Certificates of Currency available before mobilising.
3. Review the Before You Dig (BYD) information for the project and be satisfied you
   know the location of utilities. BYD spatial accuracy is often poorly constrained.
   AgriProve completes and integrates BYD into the sampling plan, but every contractor
   should independently satisfy themselves the risks have been addressed before
   sampling.
4. Complete a briefing with the landholder or farm manager for each project, covering
   access and any site-specific risks or biosecurity requirements. A record of this
   briefing is required. AgriProve can supply a template if needed.
5. Complete and retain daily vehicle and equipment pre-start checks. AgriProve does not
   require these as standard but may request copies at any time.
6. Thoroughly clean and wash down all equipment at the end of each project, to prevent
   spreading pests and disease between properties, and keep a record of the washdown.
   AgriProve can supply a template if needed.
7. If a site-specific risk or task outside normal day-to-day sampling comes up,
   complete a risk assessment before attempting it.
8. Carry adequate communications at all times, including a plan for areas with no
   mobile reception (satellite phone, Starlink, and so on).
9. Give the AgriProve Head of Operations a daily progress update. An informal phone
   call or SMS is fine.

Work respectfully while on-farm. Complete work in a timely, professional manner, keep
ground disturbance and disruption to farming operations to a minimum, and leave every
sampling site free of rubbish and in a safe condition.
