# :material-camera: Photos and Sign-off

Every core needs a fixed set of photos, plus a name, timestamp and signature on the
record. This is what lets an AgriProve auditor confirm the sample was taken correctly,
in the right place, without visiting the site themselves.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    Five required photos per core, in order: point of extraction, extraction location
    with landmarks, full core with length shown, L1 bag, L2 bag.

## Required photos

Once the core measurements are recorded, Survey123 steps through the required photos
for that core:

1. **Photo of point of extraction.** Shows the extraction point at ground surface.
2. **Photo of extraction location including landmarks.** A wider shot used by auditors
   to cross-reference the location during a site visit.
3. **Photo of full core with length indicated.** Shows the full core in the tube before
   bagging. Clearly identify the top and bottom of the core, and include a scale for
   reference.
4. **Photo of L1 sample in bag.** The bagged L1 (0 to 30cm) sample, with the field code
   clearly visible. A barcode can be scanned as the sample identifier at this step.
5. **Photo of L2 sample in bag.** The bagged L2 (30cm+) sample, with the field code
   clearly visible. A barcode can be scanned as the sample identifier at this step.

## Finishing the record

6. Under **Organisation responsible for sampling**, select the appropriate option.
   AgriProve employees select **AgriProve**, third-party contractors (including
   Precision Ag) select **Partner**.
7. Add any notes or comments needed.
8. Add the field technician's name under **Field Technician Name**.
9. Confirm the submission time.
10. Add a signature confirming the data for that core is a true and accurate record.

## Sampling Technician Statement

Once all sampling for a project area is complete, a written statement is required
confirming sampling was conducted in accordance with the Method and Supplement.

1. In Field Maps, tap anywhere within the project strata on the screen.
2. A pop-up opens with a link to the **Sampling Statement**. Tap it to open the form.
3. Enter all required details.

!!! danger "SWMS"
    Read and understand the statement in full before signing. This is a formal
    declaration to the Clean Energy Regulator, not a formality.

4. Sign at the bottom of the form, then tap the tick symbol to submit.

Once every core and the statement are complete, sync the data as covered in
[General Requirements](06-general-requirements.md).
