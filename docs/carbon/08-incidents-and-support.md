# :material-alert-circle: Incidents and Support

What to do if something goes wrong on-farm, and who to talk to.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    Damage a landholder's property or equipment? Notify the Head of Operations
    immediately and complete an incident report.

## Damage to third-party property or equipment

If sampling causes damage to landholder or other third-party equipment or property,
notify the AgriProve Head of Operations immediately with the type and extent of
damage, and complete an incident report.

Where AgriProve or a third-party contractor is at fault, AgriProve will compensate the
affected party for repairs as required. Depending on the cost and extent of the
damage, this may involve an insurance claim.
