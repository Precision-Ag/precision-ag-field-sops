# :material-sync: Updates and Sync

Field Maps works with no coverage and sends your work when it can.

!!! warning "DRAFT, pending sign-off"
    Steps marked `[CONFIRM]` have not been checked on a tablet yet. Do not rely on them in the field until they are cleared.

- Field Maps tries to update every **15 minutes**. If the tablet is on a network at that time, the update goes through by itself.
- With no coverage, keep working. Your work stays on the tablet and is sent when the tablet next connects. `[CONFIRM: check this on a tablet]`

!!! warning "WARNING"
    Do not clear the app's data, uninstall Field Maps or sign out while there is work on the tablet that has not been sent.
