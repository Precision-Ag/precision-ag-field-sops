# :material-map-search: ArcGIS Field Maps

*How to use Field Maps on the Zebra ET45, for any job type.*

!!! warning "DRAFT, pending sign-off"
    Steps marked `[CONFIRM]` have not been checked on a tablet yet. Do not rely on them in the field until they are cleared.

This book covers the app itself: finding your job, downloading it before you leave, sharing your location, working through your tasks, and syncing. It does not cover the sampling or survey method for a particular job. Use the book for your job type alongside this one.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    Download your job **before you leave** so it works offline. Turn on **location sharing** every time you open the job. Aim for GPS accuracy **under 5 m**. If you cannot reach a point, mark it **Missed** and leave a note.
