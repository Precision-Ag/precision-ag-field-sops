# :material-shovel: Collecting the Soil Sample

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    Goal: collect sufficient subsamples, combined into one, to fill the sample bag to
    at least the "Depth" line from a **minimum of 6 subsamples**, with at least **10 m**
    between samples.

## Setup

1. Label a small box with the grower name and paddock(s) you're sampling. Have your
   labelled sample bags and 3–4 extra boxes ready.
2. Navigate your vehicle to just inside the zone shown in Fieldmaps.
3. Choose your first spot.

!!! note "NOTE"
    Make sure it is between crop rows and that there is no manure within 30 cm of your
    sampling point.

## Collecting cores

1. Clear surface debris (e.g. plant matter, rocks).
2. Take a subsample with the Amity to the target depth (calibrated per
   [Determining Drill Depth](04-drill-depth.md)).
3. Move to the next location in the transect zone.
4. Continue collecting a minimum of **6 cores** at evenly spaced intervals through the
   zone shown on Fieldmaps. There must be a minimum of **10 m** between samples.
5. Double-check that the grid zone and sample bag labels match.
6. Tip soil from the bucket on the Amity into the labelled sample bag.

!!! danger "IMPORTANT"
    Soil must reach the "Depth" line on the sample bag. If it doesn't, see
    [If you don't have enough soil](#if-you-dont-have-enough-soil) below.

7. Squeeze excess air from the sample bag and seal it.
8. Move to the next grid zone.

## Remember

- Spread the 6 cores representatively across the zone, not all in one place.
- Travel diagonally across the crop rows, never along them.
- Use water only to clean the corer when needed.
- Remove any roots/stones/stubble that accumulate in the bucket before putting the
  sample in the sample bag.

## If you don't have enough soil

If the soil level is below the "Depth" line after 6 cores:

1. Stay within the grid area.
2. Collect additional cores until the bag reaches the line, with a minimum of 10 m
   between additional subsamples.
3. Make a note in Fieldmaps ("Required extra cores for volume").
