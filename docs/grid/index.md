# :material-grid: Grid Sampling

*Grid soil sampling, from finding the point to the TNT depot.*

!!! warning "DRAFT, pending sign-off"
    This manual is a first pass from the Grid Sampling Pocket Guide. Every step is
    `[CONFIRM]` until it is verified in the review meeting by the people who do the work.
    Do not rely on it in the field yet.

This book covers the grid soil sampling procedure: pre-start safety, labelling and kit,
finding the grid point, calibrating drill depth, collecting and scanning samples, and
getting boxed samples to TNT.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    Every sample bag must be labelled before you start, every sample scanned in ESRI
    before you leave the farm, and every box counted twice. TNT drop-offs are
    **Monday–Thursday only**.
