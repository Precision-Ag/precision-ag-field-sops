# :material-truck-delivery: Storage & Dispatch

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    Keep samples cold at every stage. Scan into ESRI, box and count twice, then drop off
    Monday–Thursday only, never Friday, Saturday, or Sunday.

## Sample storage (in the field and prior to dispatch)

!!! warning "WARNING"
    All strategic samples must be kept cold by storing in an insulated box with icepacks
    or in a refrigerator. Samples that warm up compromise the results.

- [ ] Store eskys or refrigerator in a cool area, vehicle shade, or indoors where
      possible.

## Scanning samples into ESRI

1. Open ESRI on the Zebra.
2. Search for the job.
3. Find the matching sample name/number.
4. Scan the barcode on the bag.
5. Repeat until all samples are scanned.
6. Sync → Submit → Sync again.

## Storing samples before postage

!!! warning "WARNING"
    All strategic samples must be kept cold by storing in an insulated box with icepacks
    or in a refrigerator. Samples that warm up compromise the results.

## Boxing samples for postage

**Small boxes**

1. Place samples into small sample boxes.
2. Write the number of samples on each small box.

**Large boxes**

1. Fit 4 small boxes into one large box.
2. On the large box write:
    - Job name(s) / Grower name
    - Total number of samples inside

**Counting + reconciliation**

1. Count all samples before putting them in boxes.
2. Count again after boxing.
3. Reconcile with the expected number in ESRI / Field Maps.
4. Do not include unused soil bags.

**Sealing**

1. Seal the large box with tape.

## Printing a consignment note

1. Provide the number of boxes and average weight of boxes to the Scheduling Manager.
   `[CONFIRM: who is the Scheduling Manager, name and contact.]`
2. Consignment note labels will be emailed to the technician.
3. Print adhesive labels via the Labelife App using the label printer.
4. Adhere labels to boxes, ensure each box has a label attached.

## Dropping off samples

- [ ] Drop off at the nearest TNT depot as soon as possible.
- [ ] **Do NOT drop off on Friday, Saturday, or Sunday.**
- [ ] If sampling finishes Friday–Sunday, keep samples with you until Monday or hand to
      another technician.
