# :material-book-open-page-variant: Introduction

This pocket guide gives you quick, practical steps to collect strategic soil samples for
Precision Ag. Follow each step to ensure samples are consistent, clean, and compliant.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    Check the site for hazards, collect samples to the depths on the job, keep them cold,
    scan them into ESRI, then box and dispatch by Thursday at the latest.

## What this manual covers

This is a field guide, short, simple, and designed for use on-the-go.

1. Run the safety checklist before you start.
2. Prep, label bags and check your kit.
3. Find the sampling location in Field Maps.
4. Collect the sample to the depths on the job.
5. Store, scan, box, and dispatch the samples.
