# :material-clipboard-check: Safety Checklist

Assess potential hazards before entering a site and/or starting sampling. Carefully
review each item below and address any safety issues identified before you start.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    Work through every item before you start sampling. If you can't answer yes, fix it
    or stop and call, see [Support](10-support.md).

## Before you start

- [ ] Have you checked the work area for any hazards or obstructions?
      Examples: other machinery and vehicles, operational activity in your area of
      work, your parking location, livestock and wildlife, steep, wet, uneven or rocky
      ground, sowed crop, tramlines and raised beds.
- [ ] Have you considered weather conditions that may affect the task?
      Examples: extreme heat, wet weather or storm activity, Total Fire Bans.
- [ ] Are you wearing the necessary PPE to protect against identified hazards?
      Examples: ear and eye protection, skin protection, no loose clothing or hair,
      appropriate sun protection, hydration and breaks.
- [ ] Have you inspected the tools and equipment for any defects?
- [ ] Are the tools and equipment in good working condition?
- [ ] Are you aware of the emergency procedures in case of an incident?
