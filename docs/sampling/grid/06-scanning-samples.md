# :material-barcode-scan: Scanning Samples into ESRI

!!! danger "IMPORTANT"
    This must be completed **before leaving the farm**, to confirm all samples have
    been collected correctly before you leave the site.

1. Open LabStream on the Zebra.
2. Sync.
3. Search for the job.
4. Find the matching sample name/number.
5. Scan the barcode on the bag.
6. Repeat until all samples are scanned and have a green tick next to them.
7. Sync → Submit → Sync again.
