# :material-crosshairs-gps: Finding the Sampling Location

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    Open Field Maps, navigate to the point, and move until you're in the correct zone.
    The transect line helps you navigate between points.

## Steps

1. Open Field Maps.
2. Navigate to the first sampling point (usually visible as a small dot).
3. Move until you are at the correct zone.

!!! note "NOTE"
    The transect line is shown to assist with navigation between points.
