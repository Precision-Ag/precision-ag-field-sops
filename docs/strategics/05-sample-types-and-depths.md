## Coring equipment

- [ ] Strategic samples are collected using either a **Daybreak** or **Christie** sampler.
- [ ] Samples may be collected using either a **20mm** or a **38mm** core. The 20mm core
      is the default width; in some soils extracting the core can be difficult and may
      be easier with the wider core.

## Types of strategic samples

There are several types of strategic samples that may be requested. Read the job notes
carefully before collection to ensure the correct procedure is followed.

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Transect — Shallow** | 20 × 0–10cm (or 0–15cm) deep samples along a transect line. Samples must be spread across the length of the transect. |
| **Transect — Deep** | 4 × deep cores collected at predetermined locations along a transect line. Samples must be collected at the exact location and logged at the time of sampling. |
| **Strategic — Single Point** | 2 × deep cores at a single location. Samples must be collected at the exact location and logged at the time of sampling. |
| **Strategic — Zone Based** | 4 × deep cores collected at predetermined locations within a zone. Samples must be collected at the exact location and logged at the time of sampling. Zones may be spread across the entire paddock. |

## Depths

Strategic samples may be collected across a range of depths, consisting of either a
single or multiple depth sample. Read the job notes carefully before collection to
ensure the correct depths are collected.

For Transect–Deep, Strategic–Single Point, and Strategic–Zone jobs, samples are
frequently collected to a depth of 60cm.

!!! note "EXAMPLE 1 — single depth"
    A Transect–Deep job requires a single depth of 0–60cm. At each red dot along the
    green line, stop and take 1 core to 60cm. Once all 4 cores are collected, combine
    them to make one sample in total.

!!! note "EXAMPLE 2 — multiple depths"
    A job requires 0–10cm, 10–30cm and 30–60cm depths. At each red dot, collect 1 deep
    core (drill to 60cm) and split it into the required depths. Then collect an
    additional 4 × 0–10cm cores around the deep core (using the pogo stick). Once all
    cores along the transect line are collected, combine the equivalent depths. The
    result is 3 samples total: 1 × 0–10cm, 1 × 10–30cm, 1 × 30–60cm.
