# :material-shield-check: Safety Checklist

Run this checklist before entering a site and/or starting sampling. Carefully review each
item and address any safety issues identified before you pick up the corer.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    If any item below is a "no," fix it before you sample, not after.

## Before you start

- [ ] Have you checked the work area for any hazards or obstructions?
      *Examples: other machinery & vehicles, operational activity in your area of work,
      your parking location, livestock & wildlife, steep, wet, uneven or rocky ground,
      sowed crop, tramlines & raised beds.*
- [ ] Have you considered weather conditions that may affect the task?
      *Examples: extreme heat, wet weather or storm activity, Total Fire Bans, etc.*
- [ ] Are you wearing the necessary PPE to protect against identified hazards?
      *Examples: ear & eye protection, skin protection, no loose clothing or hair,
      appropriate sun protection, hydration and breaks.*
- [ ] Have you inspected the tools and equipment for any defects?
- [ ] Are the tools and equipment in good working condition?
- [ ] Are you aware of the emergency procedures in case of an incident?
