# :material-package-variant-closed: Equipment Required

Before heading out, make sure you have the following kit. Pack against this list. A
missing cable or a flat 9V can cost you a whole job.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    EM38 unit, calibration block, 9V batteries, carrier connect data cable, Emlid GPS
    RTK receiver, and the Getac.

## Kit list

- [ ] **EM38 unit**
- [ ] **Calibration block** (the manufacturer-supplied block used to null the sensor, see
      [Calibration](04-calibration.md))
- [ ] **9V batteries**, bring spares. The EM38 runs on a 9V battery in Battery Mode; a flat
      battery reads outside the 720–1500 range and needs replacing before you can calibrate.
- [ ] **Clean cloth**, for wiping the sensor down before calibration.
- [ ] **Carrier connect data cable**, connects the EM38 to the towed carrier/sled for survey.
- [ ] **Power & Data cable** (DB9 connector) and a **DB9 to USB cable**, for the EM38 to
      the Getac. `[CONFIRM: same cabling as the DualEM?]`
- [ ] **Emlid Reach RS2+/RS3 cable** with DB9 connector, plus a **portable charger bank**
      and **USB-C cable**.
- [ ] **Power source for the EM38.** Your Polaris or ute will have an integrated power
      source. If not, bring a battery with an **Anderson-to-alligator-clamp** connector.
- [ ] **Emlid GPS RTK receiver** (or an **Ag Leader** GPS)
- [ ] **Getac computer** or other approved surveying device
- [ ] **Cable ties, tape or velcro**, for securing cables to the cradle. Cables break
      easily on brush.
- [ ] **Starlink**. LTE works in a pinch but is very slow and drops out, so take a Starlink
      when doing any surveying.
- [ ] **Second GPS (another Emlid or an Ag Leader) as a base station**, with a power source.
      Only for an **elevation survey** outside CORS coverage. An Emlid reaches the CORS
      network for its corrections, and about 90% of jobs are inside it.

!!! note "NOTE"
    Your ute or Polaris is already fitted with an EM cradle, the EM38 and the DualEM both
    go in it. You do not need to bring one. Wired is the standard connection. Newer
    equipment can run wirelessly if the digital team has confirmed it.

!!! tip "TIP"
    Charge the Emlid charger bank and the Getac the night before, and pack spare 9V
    batteries. Field techs lose more survey time to flat batteries than to anything else.

!!! warning "WARNING"
    Remove the 9V battery from the EM38 after each use and reinstall it before your next
    use, leaving it in flattens it between jobs.
