# :material-headset: Support

Who to call when something is not right in the field.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    For support or clarification, contact Brandon on 0472 810 174.

## Contact

- **Brandon, 0472 810 174.** First point of contact for any support or clarification on
  the EM38 survey setup, calibration, or Subsoil workflow.

## Escalation

- **Equipment malfunction** (EM38, GPS, cabling): David Johnson, 0477 770 772.
- **Systems support** (Subsoil, the Getac): Brandon, 0472 810 174.
- Gamma has its own contacts, see the Gamma book.

## Before you call

Have these ready. It gets you a faster answer.

- [ ] What step were you on? (for example, "Calibration, step 4")
- [ ] What did you expect, and what did you see? (for example, "vertical reading isn't 2× horizontal")
- [ ] Battery reading in Battery Mode
- [ ] Polaris or ute?
- [ ] Any on-screen error text or codes.

!!! note "NOTE"
    Check [Troubleshooting](07-troubleshooting.md) first. If the troubleshooting table
    does not resolve the issue, contact support immediately.
