Who to call when something is not right in the field.

## Contact

  the survey setup or the DualEM and Subsoil workflow.

## Escalation

- Gamma has its own contacts, see the Gamma book.

## Before you call

Have these ready. It gets you a faster answer.

- [ ] What step were you on? (for example, "Connect the DualEM, step 6")
- [ ] What did you expect, and what did you see? (for example, "no `$PDLS1` value")
- [ ] Wired or wireless DualEM, and which COM port?
- [ ] Polaris or ute?
- [ ] Any on-screen error text or codes.

!!! note "NOTE"
    Check [Troubleshooting](06-troubleshooting.md) first. If the troubleshooting table
    does not resolve the issue, contact support immediately.
