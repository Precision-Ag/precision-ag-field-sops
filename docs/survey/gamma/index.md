# :material-radioactive: Gamma

*Gamma radiometric survey with the RS-602 crystal pack and RadAssist.*

This is the field reference for running a Gamma survey on the Getac. Each chapter opens
with an AT A GLANCE box, then the detailed steps.

!!! danger "SWMS"
    The Gamma Crystal Pack is an extremely valuable and fragile piece of equipment.
    Minimise shock to it, never drive with it unmounted, and never travel over 25 km/h
    while it's mounted to the vehicle. See [Introduction](00-introduction.md) for the
    full list of key requirements before you head out.

2. :material-laptop: [Getac Setup](02-getac-setup.md). Mounting the Getac and setting up the job folder.
3. :material-radioactive: [Gamma Rig Setup](03-gamma-setup.md). Mounting the crystal pack, console, rover, and connecting in RadAssist.
4. :material-database-export: [Data Collection & Export](04-data-collection.md). USB backup, loading data in RadAssist, and exporting to SHP.
5. :material-archive: [Packup Procedure](05-packup.md). Pack down at the end of the job.
6. :material-wrench: [Troubleshooting](06-troubleshooting.md). Symptom, likely cause, fix.
7. :material-headset: [Support](07-support.md). Who to call, escalation, and reporting issues.
