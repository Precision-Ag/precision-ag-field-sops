# :material-archive: Packup Procedure

Pack down the gamma rig properly at the end of the job. This follows on from
[Data Collection & Export](04-data-collection.md).

!!! danger "SWMS"
    The gamma crystal pack is extremely valuable and fragile. Handle it carefully and
    always return it to its own case — never leave it mounted or loose in transit.

## Steps

1. Disconnect all cords, in order, from:
      - [ ] Getac
      - [ ] Car battery
      - [ ] Gamma crystal pack
      - [ ] Gamma console
      - [ ] Rover
2. Shut down the Getac and confirm all files have been saved.
3. Remove the bolts from the gamma crystal pack and carefully put it back into its
   travel case. Put the nuts/bolts in the box with the gamma console.
4. Place the rest of the cords and equipment back into their respective boxes — see
   [Equipment Required](01-equipment-needed.md) for what goes where.
