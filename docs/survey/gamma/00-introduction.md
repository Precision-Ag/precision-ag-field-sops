# :material-book-open-page-variant: Introduction

Set up the Gamma crystal pack and RadAssist to run gamma radiometric survey jobs in the
field. This manual is for Precision Ag field techs.

!!! info "AT A GLANCE"
    Gather the kit, mount the crystal pack and console, connect in RadAssist, run the
    survey, then back up and export straight into the job's **Survey Data** folder,
    the same one the EM survey's CSV lands in. No zip, no email. It syncs
    automatically like the rest of the job.

## What this manual covers

1. Gather and check your equipment.
2. Mount the Getac and set up today's job folder.
3. Mount the crystal pack, console and rover, and connect in RadAssist.
4. Back up data at the end of each paddock/day and export it to shapefile.
5. Pack down properly — the crystal pack is fragile and valuable.

## Key requirements

!!! danger "SWMS"
    - **Minimise shock to the gamma crystal pack.** It is an extremely valuable and
      fragile piece of equipment.
    - **Pack up all equipment at the end of each day** into their boxes to avoid damage
      in transit.
    - **Do not drive with the gamma crystal pack unmounted.**
    - **Do not travel over 25 km/h** while the crystal pack is mounted to the vehicle.
    - **Do not survey in the rain**, or until 24 hours after rain has passed.

!!! warning "WARNING"
    - If conducting an elevation survey, ensure the 12V battery has been charged the
      previous night.
    - Confirm the job is staged in **PA Survey - General** by Deal ID before heading out.
      `[CONFIRM: does gamma now use the same GIS-staged PA Survey - General folder as
      DualEM/EM38, replacing the old email-the-boundaries step? This line hasn't been
      re-checked against that change yet, don't assume either way in the field.]`
    - Ensure TeamViewer is downloaded and you have a working LTE connection on the Getac
      before heading out.
